# Achievements Engine

The **Achievements Engine** gamifies the execution layer, providing positive reinforcement to operators while simultaneously acting as high-value signals for Managers and Founders.

---

## Purpose

To celebrate wins, reinforce positive behaviors (consistency, momentum), and notify the team when operators succeed.

## Example Achievements

Achievements are unlocked via the Event Engine matching specific criteria:
- **Milestone Unlocks:** `First Outreach`, `First Client`, `Certified`.
- **Behavioral Badges:** 
  - `7-Day Consistency` (Logged in and executed tasks for 7 consecutive days).
  - `Community Contributor` (Answered 5 questions in Circle).
  - `Momentum Master` (Completed 3 missions in 48 hours).
  - `Top Performer` (Maintained an Analysis Score > 90 for 14 days).

## System Behavior

When an achievement is unlocked:
1. **Manager Notification:** Appears in the Manager's "Today View" under `Celebrate Wins` so the OSM can manually congratulate them.
2. **Operator Profile:** Permanently attached to the operator's record as a badge of honor.
3. **Founder Dashboard:** Aggregated to show Community Momentum (e.g., "15 First Clients achieved this month").
