# Operator Journey Model

The operator progression in OperatorOS is modeled as a linear sequence of verifiable milestones. Progress is measured by evidence and milestone sign-offs, not subjective opinion.

---

## The 11 Core Milestones

```text
1. Joined
   ↓
2. Onboarding
   ↓
3. Profile Complete
   ↓
4. Tools Connected
   ↓
5. First Outreach
   ↓
6. First Reply
   ↓
7. First Sales Call
   ↓
8. First Client
   ↓
9. First Campaign
   ↓
10. Case Study
   ↓
11. Certified
```

---

## Milestone Specifications

| Step | Milestone Name | Trigger / Proof | Expected Max SLA | Playbook Associated |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | `Joined` | Account provisioned & invite accepted | Day 0 | `Welcome & Setup` |
| **2** | `Onboarding` | Watched orientation video & answered baseline diagnostic | Day 2 | `Onboarding Checklist` |
| **3** | `Profile Complete` | Target niche, offer & avatar configured in profile | Day 4 | `Niche & Offer Validation` |
| **4** | `Tools Connected` | CRM, messaging accounts, & email domains linked | Day 7 | `Tech Stack Setup` |
| **5** | `First Outreach` | 25 outbound prospects contacted & logged | Day 10 | `Outreach Sprint` |
| **6** | `First Reply` | First prospect response received & categorized | Day 12 | `Objection Handling` |
| **7** | `First Sales Call` | Sales call scheduled & logged in CRM | Day 15 | `Sales Call Preparation` |
| **8** | `First Client` | Payment link executed or contract signed 🎉 | Day 21 | `Client Onboarding SOP` |
| **9** | `First Campaign` | Full campaign workflow executed for client | Day 30 | `Campaign Execution` |
| **10** | `Case Study` | Written/video client testimonial published | Day 45 | `Case Study Blueprint` |
| **11** | `Certified` | Operator meets all success KPIs & gets certified | Day 60 | `Mastery & Scaling` |

---

## SLA & Delay Trigger Logic
If an operator stays in any single stage longer than **1.5x the Expected Max SLA**, the Risk Engine automatically fires a **Milestone Latency Risk (+30)** signal to the OSM.
