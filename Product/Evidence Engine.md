# Evidence Engine

The **Evidence Engine** replaces the vanity metric of "Course Completion" by shifting the focus entirely to execution. We don't track what an operator watched; we track what they *did*.

---

## Purpose

Capture execution evidence to validate that an operator is truly advancing, rather than just clicking "mark as complete" on lessons.

## Evidence Sources

Evidence can be submitted manually by the operator or captured automatically via the Event Engine:
- **Circle Posts:** Linking to a win or submission in the community forum.
- **Assignments:** PDFs, spreadsheets, or forms.
- **Videos/Documents/Screenshots:** Proof of setup (e.g. DNS configuration screenshot).
- **Coach Approval:** Direct manual validation by the OSM.
- **Community Participation:** Attending live events or helping others.

## Engine Impacts

When Evidence is submitted and validated, it automatically updates:
1. **The Journey Engine:** Unlocking the next stage.
2. **The Analysis Score:** Increasing execution health.
3. **The Risk Score:** Decreasing risk.
4. **Mission Progress:** Checking off mission milestones.
5. **Operator Timeline:** Creating an immutable log of execution for the Coach and Manager.
