# Analysis Engine

The **Analysis Engine** replaces the traditional, one-dimensional "Course Progress %" with a dynamic, multi-faceted score. 

---

## The Analysis Score (0-100)

The Analysis Score represents the Operator's overall execution health, consistency, and momentum. It is calculated dynamically from multiple signals.

### Purpose
- **For the Operator:** It serves as motivation and a clear indicator that they are "on track" or falling behind.
- **For the Manager:** It acts as a decision-making tool, mirroring the Risk Score but framed positively (higher Analysis Score = lower Risk Score).

### Signal Inputs

The engine aggregates data from various sources to calculate the score:

1. **Circle/Skool Logins:** Frequency of accessing the learning platform.
2. **Course Completion:** Deep-link completion events from the LMS.
3. **Mission Completion:** Finishing structured execution blocks within OperatorOS.
4. **Community Participation:** Activity level in external forums.
5. **Event Attendance:** Showing up to mastermind and Q&A calls.
6. **Coaching Feedback:** Positive or constructive notes from the assigned coach.
7. **Assignment Completion:** Uploading trackers, scripts, or evidence.
8. **Journey Milestones:** Advancing through the 11 stages within SLA parameters.
9. **Consistency & Inactivity:** Penalties applied for gaps in execution or login streaks.

---

## Shared Data Model

Both the Operator and the Manager view the same underlying data, but through different lenses.

- **Operator View:** Sees their personal Analysis Score, their current stage, and the specific actions required to improve it.
- **Manager View:** Sees the Analysis Score alongside internal Risk Scores, hidden coach notes, suggested AI interventions, and recovery recommendations.
