# Knowledge Engine Specification

The Knowledge Engine turns operational interactions into reusable, searchable assets automatically.

---

## The Knowledge Pipeline Architecture

```text
1. Operator Question
   ↓
2. OSM Answer / Call Teardown
   ↓
3. Video Recording
   ↓
4. Automated Transcript Extraction
   ↓
5. AI Summary & Key Takeaways
   ↓
6. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Auto-Draft
   ↓
7. Verified Knowledge Base & Playbook Asset
```

---

## Core Artifact Types

### 1. Q&A Record
- **Source**: Submitted via operator portal or extracted from call.
- **Fields**: Question, Operator Name, Date, Stage, Tag, Video Timestamp, Verified Answer.

### 2. Video Call Recording
- **Source**: Mastermind calls, 1-on-1 interventions, loom teardowns.
- **Fields**: Title, Duration, Embedded Player URL, AI Chapter Markers, Full Transcript.

### 3. AI Summary
- **Source**: Generated by LLM processing transcripts.
- **Fields**: Problem Statement, Core Strategy, Key Steps, Common Mistakes to Avoid.

### 4. Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
- **Source**: Auto-compiled from top-rated AI Summaries after OSM approval.
- **Fields**: SOP ID, Title, Stage Alignment, Step-by-Step Checklist, Downloads/Templates.

---

## Semantic Search & Tagging
Knowledge assets are auto-indexed by:
- **Journey Stage** (`First Outreach`, `Sales Call`, `Offer Pricing`)
- **Operator Segment** (B2B SaaS, Agency, E-commerce)
- **Topic Tags** (`Copywriting`, `Cold Email`, `Stripe Setup`, `CRM Workflow`)
