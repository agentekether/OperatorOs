# Vision & Product Vision

OperatorOS is a **Customer Success Platform for Transformation Communities**.

While platforms like Circle and Skool handle the learning and community layer, OperatorOS acts as the **execution, coaching, and intelligence layer**. It does not duplicate community features; instead, it interprets community activity to drive execution.

## The Problem

Traditional community platforms focus on engagement, content consumption, and vanity metrics (like video completion percentage). This leaves coaching teams blind to actual execution and implementation. Operators get stuck, churn increases, and success rates remain opaque.

## The OperatorOS Solution

OperatorOS transforms community participation into measurable member success by capturing standardized events from integration platforms (Circle, Zoom, Skool) and feeding them into intelligent engines (Journey, Risk, Evidence, Analysis, and Achievements). 

> **North Star**: Every feature must strengthen the core philosophy of transforming community participation into measurable outcomes. If a feature duplicates Circle, we replace it with an integration or intelligence layer instead.

## Core Product Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Risk Lead Time** | Time between an operator falling off track and the OSM receiving a risk signal. | `< 24 hours` |
| **Intervention Efficiency** | % of flagged operators who return to active stage within 5 days of intervention. | `> 75%` |
| **Playbook Resolution Rate** | % of recurring operator bottlenecks resolved via standard SOPs vs custom manual effort. | `> 80%` |
