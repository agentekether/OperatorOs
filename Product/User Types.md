# User Types & Personas

OperatorOS serves three distinct primary user types within an operator ecosystem or cohort-based community.

---

## 1. The Operator (Cohort Member)

### Profile
An entrepreneur, builder, or operator executing a playbook inside the accelerator/community.

### Core Goals
- Make tangible progress without getting stuck or overwhelmed.
- Know exactly what step to take next.
- Access instant, verified answers and playbooks when facing an operational barrier.

### Key Pain Points
- Imposter syndrome or paralysis when facing unfamiliar tasks (e.g., first sales outreach).
- Feeling lost in a noisy chat community (Slack/Discord/Skool).

### Product Needs in OperatorOS
- Visual milestone journey map.
- Frictionless Q&A submission.
- One-click access to Playbooks & SOPs.

---

## 2. The Operator Success Manager (OSM) — Primary Persona

### Profile
The operational leader tasked with driving cohort outcomes, accountability, and member retention.

### Core Goals
- Supervise 50-100+ operators concurrently.
- Intervene early before an operator drops out or fails.
- Systematize community knowledge into reusable playbooks.

### Key Pain Points
- Spending hours in 1-on-1 calls repeating the exact same advice.
- Finding out an operator quit 3 weeks after they stopped engaging.

### Product Needs in OperatorOS
- `Today` screen prioritizing high-risk operators and key wins.
- Deep-dive `Operator Profile` with complete timeline & suggested interventions.
- Playbook runner & Knowledge Engine management.

---

## 3. The Founder / System Designer

### Profile
The founder of the program, community, or accelerator who owns business outcomes and system architecture.

### Core Goals
- Know if the operating system behind the program is working.
- Identify friction points in the curriculum or playbook.
- Scale cohort capacity without ballooning support headcount.

### Key Pain Points
- Lack of visibility into why members fail or drop out.
- Dependence on key individual OSMs rather than scalable processes.

### Product Needs in OperatorOS
- Ecosystem macro health analytics.
- Risk distribution by stage.
- Playbook completion & bottleneck diagnostics.
